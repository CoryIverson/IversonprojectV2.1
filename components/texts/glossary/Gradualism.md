---

Gradualism is an idea that large changes in nature arise through small, steady processes over massive periods of time. The theory stems from Charles Lyell's Principles of Geology. In short,it argued against sudden leaps or dramatic shifts, and argues that the accumulation of countless tiny alterations gradually shapes the world we see. This principle was central to nineteenth-century geology and later became a cornerstone of Darwin's and others evolutionary theories.
