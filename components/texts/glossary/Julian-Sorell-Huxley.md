A British evolutionary biologist, writer, and public educator, and the grandson of Thomas Henry Huxley. Julian Huxley was central to the development and popularzation of the “Modern Synthesis,” which combined Darwinian natural selection with genetics in the early twentieth century. He was also a major figure in public science communication, serving as the first Director-General of UNESCO and the World Wildlife Fund(WWF). His influence extended across evolutionary theory, conservation, and popular science writing.
