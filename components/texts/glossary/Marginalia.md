---

Marginalia refers to notes, marks, underlining, or comments written in the margins of a book or document. These annotations provide valuable evidence of how readers interpreted, questioned, or engaged with a text. In archival research, marginalia can reveal a reader’s thought process, disagreements, or evolving ideas ; with each mark, underline, and comment possibly answering historical questions, raising new ones, and at times leading to a cold case. 
 
Example: A scientist writing comments next to a passage in On the Origin of Species to compare Darwin’s ideas with their own.
