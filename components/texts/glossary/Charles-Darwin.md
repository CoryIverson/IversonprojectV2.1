---

A British naturalist best known for developing the theory of evolution by natural selection or as it was orignalY called "common descent with modification". His 1859 book On the Origin of Species transformed how scientists understand biological change, and created a theory of thought that changed the discourse of science, politics, and religion to this day. Darwin’s work drew on decades of observations, correspondence, and collaboration with a wide network of researchers, collectors, colleagues, and critics. Yet, the importance of his theory can not be understated and there is a reason that Charles Darwin, is a household name even to this day.
