title: title: UCSB's Special Research Collection
---
[The Special Research Collection](https://www.library.ucsb.edu/special-collections) at UCSB is the department that overseas the archives at UCSB, as they state:
>"The Department of Special Research Collections acquires, preserves, and makes accessible rare, valuable, or unique materials that support UC Santa Barbara students, faculty, and research programs, as well as the scholarly community. 

Although some of these materials are digitized and available remotely, many are not or can't be, and the experience of the archive is often impossible to recreate digitally. For example, holding a real first edition copy of Charles Darwin's Orgin of Species creates a mental and emotional response that is impossible to recreate, and other items like a 19th century phonograph is impossible to create a compelling digital experience.
