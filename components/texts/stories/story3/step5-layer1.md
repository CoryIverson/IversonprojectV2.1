---
title: Critcal works & the impact of UNESCO.
---
Julian’s most significant contribution was his 1942 book, *Evolution: The Modern Synthesis*, which was one of many into a cannon of works that began a unification of Darwinian natural selection with the new science of genetics which created this [[modern-synthesis]] of evolution. This framework shifted the scientific focus away from individual lineages and toward changes in gene frequencies within populations, integrating fields like paleontology and zoology into a cohesive whole. Crucially, this new understanding helped disentangle genetics from the flawed pseudoscience of Eugenics, a shift that Julian championed through [[unesco]] by promoting a scientifically accurate, anti-racist understanding of human variation.
