---
title: Critcal works & the impact of UNESCO.
---
Julian’s most significant contribution was his 1942 book, *Evolution: The Modern Synthesis*, which gave a name to the unification of Darwinian natural selection with the new science of genetics. This framework shifted the scientific focus away from individual lineages and toward changes in gene frequencies within populations, integrating fields like paleontology and zoology into a cohesive whole. Crucially, this new understanding helped disentangle genetics from the flawed pseudoscience of Eugenics, a shift that Julian championed through UNESCO by promoting a scientifically accurate, anti-racist understanding of human variation.
