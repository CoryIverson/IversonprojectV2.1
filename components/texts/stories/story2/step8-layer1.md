title: The Darwin Correspondence Project
---
Many of the resources, images and links were taken from The Darwin Correspondence Project. 

[The Darwin Correspondence Project](https://www.darwinproject.ac.uk/) is an ongoing scholarly effort to collect, transcribe, and publish the thousands of letters Charles Darwin exchanged with scientists, family members, readers, and critics throughout his life. These letters reveal Darwin not as an isolated thinker, but as someone embedded in a dense network of conversations that shaped his ideas. For this exhibit, the project matters because it shows the very networks of Huxley, Wallace, and many others vis-à-vis Darwin that this UCSB collection also reflects. Together, they help us see evolution not as a single theory evolved through the decades but an entire revolution of many theories.
