---
title: Darwins Theory of Evolution
---
Darwin's theory, by the time of his biggest book *On the Origin of Species by Means of Natural Selection, or the Preservation of Favoured Races in the Struggle for Life* (1859), was not so dissimilar to what many of us think of when we think of evolution, but some of its biggest concepts, as we know them today, were brought forth not from this book but from public and private discourse both before and after the book.
