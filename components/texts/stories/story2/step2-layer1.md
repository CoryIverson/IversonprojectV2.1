Darwin's theory by the time of his biggest book *On The Orgin of Species by Means of Natural Selection, on the Preservation of Favoured races in the Struggle for Life* (1859) was not so disimilar to what many of us think of when we think of Evolution, but some of its biggest concepts as we know them today were brought forth not from this book, but from public and private discourse both before and after the book.
