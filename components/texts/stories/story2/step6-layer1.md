title: Darwinism vs Darwin
---
Social Darwinism took the popular theory (Darwin's Theory) and was distorted via political, psuedoscientific and popularist theories from figures like Herbert Spencer, the founder of Eugenics (and Darwin's Cousin) Francis Galton, and various other influences at the time.Charles Darwin himself was not a supporter of these ideals, but it stuck for a variety of reasons. One of which is simply that in historizing the term and evolutionary history many historians such as Richard Hofstadter and figures at the time cemented the term into the zeitgeist. 
>"Today, most modern biologists reject the phrase because 'fittest' has connotations in popular culture well beyond its original biological meaning of reproductive success. It was used in the 19th century by so-called social Darwinists to justify the unrestrained competition of cut-throat capitalism, and by creationists in the 20th century to criticise evolutionary theory for justifying moral standards that lead to ill treatment of the weak in society."
